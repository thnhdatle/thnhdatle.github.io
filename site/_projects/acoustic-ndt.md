## Problem

Voids, cracks and delamination inside a wafer or a stacked package cannot be seen from the surface. Scanning acoustic microscopy reveals them, but each scan produces a large stream of signals that has to be acquired, processed and read quickly, and manual review is slow and varies from one engineer to another.

## Approach

The platform covers the whole path from the scanner to the defect report.

- **Acquisition.** Real-time data acquisition and signal processing, with live visualization so operators can follow the inspection as it runs.
- **Data pipeline.** An acoustic data pipeline that processes scans at high throughput and feeds the analysis stage.
- **AI analysis.** A segmentation model that localizes defects automatically and integrates directly into the pipeline.

## My role

Solution architect. I designed the software and AI system, and researched and developed its acquisition, data pipeline and AI analysis layers. The scanner's mechanics and automation control are built by other teams.

## Status

In active research and development, with ongoing improvements across the system.
