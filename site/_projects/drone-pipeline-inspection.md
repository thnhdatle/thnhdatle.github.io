## Problem

Pipelines are long, often buried, and cross terrain that is hard to reach. Inspecting them on foot is slow, and the measurements it produces still have to be interpreted by hand.

## Approach

The system covers the whole path from the drone to the anomaly report.

- **Measurement.** Magnetic signals collected from a drone along the pipeline.
- **Data pipeline.** A pipeline that processes and analyzes the signals.
- **AI analysis.** A model that detects anomalies along the pipeline and localizes them automatically.

## My role

Technical lead. I own the architecture of the software and AI system, from the drone measurements to the anomaly report, and lead it from design to field testing.

## Status

In testing. The videos below only illustrate drone-based pipeline inspection by other companies. They are not footage of this system.
