I am a Technical Lead at [OHLabs](https://www.ohlabs.co.kr) and a graduate researcher at NBM Lab, Pukyong National University, advised by Prof. Junghwan Oh.

At OHLabs, I lead the AI team developing efficient and reliable LLM infrastructure. I also build ultrasonic inspection systems for semiconductor applications and industrial use.

At NBM Lab, my research focuses on efficient AI systems, from developing compact models to enabling large-model inference on resource-constrained hardware.

Before my current roles, I spent five years in software engineering, working primarily on distributed systems, large-scale data processing, and backend infrastructure.

I am the creator of five open-source projects: [Footnote](/projects/footnote/), [Triage](/projects/triage/), [Foreman](/projects/foreman/), [Loupe](/projects/loupe/), and [Wake](/projects/wake/). Together, they form an open-source ecosystem exploring lightweight, self-hosted infrastructure for LLMs and data systems.
